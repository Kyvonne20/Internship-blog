01 / The beginning

First things first... what did I get myself into?

When you hear the word internship, what do you imagine? Maybe sitting in an office all day, drinking coffee, answering emails, and trying to look busy when you have nothing to do?

Well, I can't say that's what my internship has been like. I've been making social media content, filming videos, playing Among Us at work, and dressing up as a ghost. Pretty normal, right?

I'm a third-year B-COM student at the University of Twente, and I'm currently doing my internship at J58. As an international student, starting somewhere new was quite a big step for me.

I'm also quite an introverted person. I usually listen more than I talk. Sometimes I think about what I want to say for so long that the conversation has already moved on. So, naturally, starting an internship with new people came with a few questions in my head.

Would I fit in? Would people understand my ideas? What if I didn't know what I was doing?

Spoiler alert: I didn't have everything figured out. And that's kind of the point.
02 / Meet the company

So... what exactly is J58?

J58 develops standing wheelchairs. Yes, standing wheelchairs! Before starting here, I didn't really know much about this world. Now, it's part of my everyday internship life.

I'm working as a Marketing Intern, mainly focusing on digital communication and social media content.

At first, I thought social media work would mostly be about making posts, taking pictures and creating videos. But there is actually a lot happening before something gets posted online.

And that's where my internship really started to get interesting.

A little reminder: marketing is not just pressing the "post" button.
03 / The work bits

Wait... there's more to social media than posting?

One of the things I've been learning is how much thinking goes into creating social media content.

Before making content, I first look at the existing social media channels, research the target audience and check what competitors are doing. This helps me understand what is already there and where there might be room for improvement.

I've also worked on a social media audit, content pillars and a content calendar. After the planning comes the more creative part: thinking of ideas, writing scripts, filming, editing and preparing content to post.

Sometimes coming up with an idea is harder than it sounds. There are moments when I just stare at my screen, hoping a brilliant idea will magically appear. Unfortunately, my screen has never helped me with that.

But I genuinely enjoy this process. I like thinking about how to turn an idea into something people might actually want to watch. And I like that the work is not always the same. Some days involve planning, other days involve filming or editing.

A little behind the scenes 📸
04 / Unexpected office activities

Plot twist: my office has impostors?!

Something I didn't expect from an internship was how much the people around me would become part of the experience.

At J58, we usually have team activities at least once a month. One of my favourites was playing Among Us in real life!

Everyone dressed in a colour they chose, and we had to complete different tasks around the office area while trying to figure out who the impostors were. It was basically the game, but with actual people running around.

For someone who is usually a little shy in a new group, activities like this made it easier to connect with everyone. I didn't have to think too much about what to say. We were just playing, laughing and trying to catch the impostors.

Crewmates? Impostors? Still not sure...

07 / How it all started

But... how did I actually end up at J58?

Funny enough, I found the internship vacancy on LinkedIn.

I was looking for an opportunity to learn more about marketing, and the position at J58 caught my attention. So I applied, got invited for an interview, and met two people from the company.

One of the interviewers was Romanian. My boyfriend is also Romanian, so that small connection made the conversation feel a little more familiar. It helped me feel more comfortable and made it easier to connect.

Of course, I was still nervous. I wasn't completely sure if I was good enough or if I knew enough. But I decided to give it a try.

And that brings me to something I made during this internship...

And the last word is the one I want you to remember.

TRY.
08 / A note to future interns

So... just TRY.

Looking back, I think I spent quite a lot of time worrying before I even started. Was my CV good enough? Would I fit in? Was I choosing the right company? Would I be able to do the work?

I didn't have all the answers. I still don't. But applying gave me the chance to find out.

10 / Halfway through the story

So... can you do a wheelie?

I'm halfway through my internship now, and I've already learned more than I expected. I've learned about social media planning, content creation, filming and editing. I've also learned what it's like to work with a team, share ideas and slowly become more comfortable in a new environment.

I'm still introverted. I still overthink sometimes. And I definitely don't have everything figured out.

But I think that's okay. A good internship isn't about becoming a completely different person. It's about finding a place where you can be yourself, try things, make mistakes, ask questions and grow.
